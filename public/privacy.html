Privacy Policy

This website lets guests of the Movie Character Night event share photos and videos. It is run privately by the event organiser.

What we collect

The photos and videos you upload, and the name you type in (optional). We do not ask you to sign in, and we do not use cookies or tracking.

Where it is stored

Uploads are stored in the organiser's Google Drive. Each upload is shared as "anyone with the link can view" so it can appear in the event gallery, which anyone with the event link can see.

Google account access

The app uses only the organiser's own Google account, with the drive.file permission. That permission lets it manage only the files and folders the app created itself, and nothing else in that Drive. Guests' Google accounts are never accessed.

Removal

To have one of your uploads removed, contact the event organiser and it will be deleted from Google Drive.
